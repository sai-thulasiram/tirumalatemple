Karthika Pournami Deepotsavam 2026 at Tirumala

Date: 24 Nov 2026 (Tuesday). TTD has not confirmed this date yet; it is the expected date from the panchang.

Expected crowd: Busy. Last time (5 Nov 2025), about 63,239 devotees a day had darshan, 9% quieter than the two weeks around it. The free queue was up to 18 hours.

₹300 tickets for November were released on 24 Aug 2026 and are usually sold out; use an SSD token, the footpath, SRIVANI or the free queue.

About Karthika Pournami Deepotsavam

Full moon of Karthika, with lamps lit across the temple.

Tithi on 24 Nov 2026: Purnima, Shukla paksha, Kartika masam (at sunrise in Tirumala).

Planning tips

  • Stay in Tirupati and go up for the day if rooms on the hill are full; free lockers are near the queue complexes.
  • Expect queues to start building the evening before and to stay long for a day after.
  • Check today's queue on the morning you travel.
