TTD ₹300 darshan ticket release date for April 2027

₹300 Special Entry Darshan tickets for April 2027 are expected to be released on Sunday, 24 January 2027 at 10:00 AM IST on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in.

Rooms in Tirumala and Tirupati for April open the same day at 3:00 PM. TTD releases each month's quota about three months ahead; it confirms exact dates a few days before, so check TTD's notice.

If tickets are already sold out

  • SSD time-slot token (free): collect it in Tirupati the day before at Srinivasam (near the central bus stand), Vishnu Nivasam (opposite the railway station) or the Bhudevi Complex (Alipiri), in person with original Aadhaar.
  • Walk up by Srivari Mettu or Alipiri; walkers get a Divya Darshan token on the way.
  • SRIVANI (₹10,000 donation + ₹500 ticket per person): same-day tickets released online around 9 AM; a small offline quota at Tirupati airport.
  • Free Sarva Darshan: no ticket needed; see today's queue.
