    Which darshan is right for me?

    There are several ways to have darshan of Sri Venkateswara Swamy. Most are free. Rules change from time to time, so confirm on TTD's site before you go.

    • Short on timeBook ₹300 Special Entry Darshan online, or get a free SSD time-slot token in Tirupati.
    • Travelling with parents over 65Book the free senior citizens quota when it opens each month.
    • Walking up the hillAsk for a Divya Darshan token on the footpath.
    • Flexible and patientJoin the free Sarva Darshan queue. Check the expected wait on this page first.

    Sarva Darshan

    The free general queueFree · no booking

    Walk into the Vaikuntam Queue Complex in Tirumala and wait your turn. Food, water and milk are given in the queue.

    Wait ranges from a few hours to more than a day. This page shows how long it was each morning.

    SSD time-slot token

    Slotted Sarva DarshanFree · token in Tirupati

    Collect a token with a darshan time at TTD's counters in Tirupati: Srinivasam, Vishnu Nivasam and the Bhudevi Complex. Carry the original Aadhaar of every adult.

    Tokens are limited each day and TTD pauses them on very crowded days.

    ₹300 Special Entry

    Special Entry Darshan (SED)₹300 per person · book online

    Book a time slot on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in. Tickets for a month go on sale about three months ahead and sell out fast. Traditional dress is required.

    See "When do tickets go on sale?" below for the exact day and time.

    Divya Darshan

    For pilgrims who walk upFree · token on the footpath

    Climb by the Alipiri or Srivari Mettu steps. TTD gives walkers a darshan token on the way. Counters and timings change, so ask at the start of the path.

    A walk of several hours; start early and carry water.

    Senior citizens and differently abled

    Dedicated quotaFree · book online

    A separate darshan slot for those aged 65 and above and for differently abled devotees, released online each month. Carry age proof or the disability certificate.

    Opens on the 23rd at 3 PM, about three months ahead.

    Sevas and SRIVANI

    Arjitha sevas and the SRIVANI TrustPaid · book online

    Sevas such as Suprabhatam and Kalyanotsavam are allotted by an online lucky dip or first come, first served. SRIVANI darshan is for donors to the SRIVANI Trust.

    Both open each month around the 19th to the 23rd.

      On the day of darshan

      A simple checklist for first-time visitors.

      1. Reach TirumalaTake a TTD or APSRTC bus or a taxi from Tirupati, about an hour up the ghat road, or walk up the footpath. Vehicles and bags are checked at Alipiri.
      2. Leave phones and bagsMobile phones, cameras and large bags are not allowed inside the temple. Use the free deposit counters near the queue complex.
      3. Dress traditionallyMen: dhoti, or kurta-pyjama, or shirt and trousers. Women: saree, half-saree or churidar with dupatta. Required for ₹300 and seva tickets.
      4. Carry IDBring the original Aadhaar of everyone named on your ticket or token. Report at your slot time.
      5. Tonsure, if you have a vowHair offering is free at the Kalyanakatta halls. Most pilgrims do it before darshan and bathe after.
      6. Laddu and AnnaprasadamCollect laddu prasadam at the laddu counters after darshan; extra laddus can be bought. Free meals are served at the Annaprasadam complex all day.

      Words you will see

      Compartments
      Waiting halls in the Vaikuntam Queue Complex. When all 31 are full, the queue continues outside.
      "Outside line at Silathoranam"
      The queue has run out of the halls and along the road. Silathoranam, ATGH and Krishna Teja guest house are nearby landmarks; Octopus building and Bata Gangamma temple mean a very long line.
      Direct line
      No waiting in the halls. Devotees walk straight through.
      Quota
      The batch of tickets TTD releases online for a month, usually three months ahead.
      Srivari hundi
      The offering box in the temple. TTD reports the cash counted each day.
      Kalyanakatta
      The halls where devotees offer their hair (tonsure).
      Annaprasadam
      Free meals TTD serves to every pilgrim.
      Brahmotsavam and Garuda Seva
      The temple's biggest annual festival and its most crowded evening procession.
      Vaikuntha Dwara darshan
      Ten days around Vaikuntha Ekadasi when devotees pass through the sacred northern gate. Usually by special token only.
